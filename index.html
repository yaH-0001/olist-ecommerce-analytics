Olist E-Commerce Analytics

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project built with Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI-ready data modeling, analyzing 99K+ orders and 96K+ customers. The project explores revenue, customer behavior, product and seller performance, payment patterns, geography, delivery performance, and customer experience. The standout finding: ~8.1% of delivered orders arrived late — a clear, quantified opening to improve customer retention and operational performance.
